# Monsters Clash — Elemental Tower Defence

Continuous escalating rounds; protect the player tower, defeat a finite enemy wave with a final elite or boss, then advance to another map automatically. Each kill earns temporary Battle Coins for in-run upgrades. Each cleared round awards both Battle Coins and persistent Menu Coins for tower/monster/team-slot unlocks. Battle Coins reset on a new run; existing saved coins remain Menu Coins. Round progression automatically scales allied monster HP and attack. Tower shield replenishes and tower HP resets on each new map; purchased run upgrades persist until restart or defeat. All six maps and environmental effects remain active. Day/Night alternates every 30 seconds. See `progression.json`, `towers.json`, `battle_rules.json`, `roster.json` and `data/maps/` for authoritative values. Only artwork-ready monsters spawn or can be unlocked. Existing profile coins and owned team are preserved. No cross-device saves.

Support drone artwork path: `assets/towers/support-drone-sheet.png` (four horizontal HP-stage frames, pending asset; only healthy frame used until drone damage is implemented). Tower range caps at 425 logical units. Elites have 150% of normal round HP and attack and 200% sprite size.
